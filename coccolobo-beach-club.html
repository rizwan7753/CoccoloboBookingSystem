Check availability

On one of the quieter stretches of St. Kitts

Coccolobo takes its name from the sea grape — the low, round-leafed tree that holds the sand together along this shore and gives the beach its shade.

The club is built around a simple idea: decide what kind of day you want before you get here, and let us have it ready. That might be a chair and an umbrella for four hours. It might be a private cabana with your own server and an open bar. It might be an afternoon learning to roll sushi, or tasting your way through Kittitian cooking with a recipe card to take home.

Because every experience is prepared for a set number of guests, excursions close for booking the evening before and walk-ins aren't accepted. Beach chairs are the exception — reserve one on the morning itself if the weather turns good.
