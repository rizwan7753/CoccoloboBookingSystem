Running Cocolobo, from zero

Two short guides in one place: how staff run the booking system day to day, and what a guest sees when they book an excursion, a beach chair, or event tickets. No prior knowledge assumed.

Staff manual

Running the booking system

01 Logging in

Every staff action happens through the admin panel, kept separate from the public site.

  1. Go to /admin/login on your booking site's address (e.g. yourdomain.com/admin/login).
  2. Enter your email and password, then select Sign in.
  3. You'll land on the Dashboard. The left sidebar is how you get everywhere else.
First time in? If you were given one of the demo accounts, change its password immediately under Staff & roles — demo passwords are not safe to leave in place on a live site.

02 The dashboard

Your landing page after login — a quick read on what's happening today and this month.

  • Stat cards summarise today's and upcoming bookings across excursions, chairs, and events.
  • The calendar shows every booking type on the date it happens — click a day to jump straight to it.

03 Excursions

Tours and activities guests book by date and departure time.

  1. Sidebar → Excursions → New excursion (or open an existing one to edit).
  2. Fill in title, description, what's included/excluded, and what to bring.
  3. Choose a pricing type: Per guest (adult/child rates) or Flat rate (one price for the whole booking, e.g. a private cabana for up to 4).
  4. Set duration, default capacity, and the booking cut-off time (how late guests can book before the departure).
  5. Add a departure time and the days of the week it runs.
  6. Optionally add a card image (shown on the listing) and a header image (shown at the top of the detail page).
  7. Set status to Active so it appears on the public site, then save.

04 Beach chair rentals

Same-day chair reservations, organised into spots (rows) and time slots.

  1. Sidebar → Rentals → create or open an item (e.g. "Beach Chair").
  2. Set the session duration — this is used to auto-generate time slots.
  3. Open the item and add spots (e.g. "Row A"), each with a chair quantity — several guests can share one spot's pool of chairs.
  4. Add time slots one at a time, or use Generate slots to fill a day automatically from an operating window and the duration you set.
  5. Each spot's remaining chairs are calculated live per date and time slot — no manual capacity tracking needed.

05 Events

One-off ticketed nights — a party, a class, a tasting.

  1. Sidebar → Events → New event.
  2. Set the date, start/end time, venue, and description.
  3. Open the event and add one or more ticket tiers (e.g. "General Admission" and "VIP"), each with its own price and capacity.
  4. Add card and header images the same way as excursions.

06 Bookings & payments

Three list pages — one per booking type — each showing upcoming bookings by default.

  • Bookings, Rental Bookings, and Event Bookings in the sidebar each list every booking of that type from today onward. Narrow by excursion/item/event or by date range using the filters at the top.
  • Select Cancel on a row to release its capacity back to availability.
  • Select Export to Excel to download the current filtered list as a spreadsheet.
Offline payments (bank transfer) — when a guest chooses to pay by bank transfer, their booking is created but held as Pending. It will not confirm on its own. Once you've verified the payment arrived (guests are asked to email their receipt referencing the booking ID), find the booking and select Mark as paid. This confirms the booking and emails the guest a confirmation. This button only appears for pending offline bookings.

07 Closures & holidays

Block a date across some or all booking types at once — no need to edit every excursion.

  1. Sidebar → Holidays → add a date and a label (e.g. "Staff training day").
  2. Choose which booking types it applies to: excursions, rentals, events — any combination.
  3. Guests see a "closed" notice on that date and can't book it; already-made bookings aren't affected automatically.

08 Settings

System-wide configuration, organised into tabs. Super Admin access only.

TabWhat it controls
GeneralBooking system name, timezone, currency — shown across the site and emails.
Email (SMTP)The mail server used to send confirmations. Send a test email once configured to confirm it works.
StripeTurn card payments on/off, and set the publishable key, secret key, and webhook signing secret. Changes apply immediately — no redeploy needed.
Offline PaymentTurn bank-transfer payment on/off, set the instructions guests see (account details), and the email address guests send their payment receipt to.

If both Stripe and Offline Payment are on, guests choose one at checkout. If only one is on, it's used automatically with no choice shown.

09 Staff & roles

Every staff member gets a role that controls what they can see and change.

RoleCan do
Super AdminEverything, including Settings and managing other staff accounts.
Location ManagerManage excursions, rentals, events, holidays, and bookings.
Booking StaffView and manage bookings — cancel, mark offline payments as paid.
FinanceView bookings and export reports; read-only, can't cancel or edit.

Manage accounts under sidebar → Users (Super Admin only). Every booking-affecting action is recorded in the Audit Log with who did it and when.

Guest manual

Booking your visit

01 Finding something to book

Three kinds of things to book, each on its own page.

  • Excursions — the homepage lists tours and activities with a set departure time.
  • Beach Chairs — same-day chair reservations by the water.
  • Events — one-off ticketed nights, listed by date.

Select any card to see full details, photos, and pricing before booking.

02 Making a booking

Every booking follows the same three steps, shown at the top of the booking box.

  1. Select — pick a date and time (excursions/chairs) or a ticket type and quantity (events).
  2. Your details — name, email, phone, and room/villa number if you're a resident guest.
  3. Payment — pay by card, or by bank transfer if that's offered.
A greyed-out time or date means it's sold out or closed — pick another. If a whole date shows as closed, the property is unavailable that day.

03 Paying

What you see depends on what the property has turned on.

  • If only card payment is offered, you'll go straight to a secure card form after entering your details.
  • If both card and bank transfer are offered, you'll be asked to choose before continuing.

04 Paying by bank transfer

If you choose bank transfer, your spot is held — but not yet confirmed.

  1. As soon as you choose bank transfer, the account details you need to pay to appear on screen.
  2. After submitting, you'll see the same details again along with your booking ID, plus an email address to send your payment receipt to.
  3. Send your receipt to that address, mentioning your booking ID, so staff can match it to your booking.
  4. You'll get an email confirming your booking once staff verify the payment arrived.

05 Your confirmation

What happens once your booking goes through.

  • You're taken to a confirmation page showing your booking reference, date/time, and details — save this page or note the reference.
  • A confirmation email is sent with the same details, plus what to bring and where to meet, if relevant.
  • Paid by bank transfer? The page and email instead say pending until staff verify your payment — a second email follows once confirmed.
